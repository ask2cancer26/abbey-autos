Insurance & accident repairs

Accident repairs, handled properly

If your car's been in an accident, we can deal with your insurer directly and carry out the repair in our own body shop — no waiting months for an approved repairer. Every repair is fully warranted and returned to pre-accident condition.

About

Three generations on the same street

Abbey Autos opened at 1–2 Hillreach in 1968, and we've been here ever since — a family-run business serving Woolwich and SE London across 58 years and three generations of customers.

That kind of longevity only comes one way: work done properly, priced fairly, and explained honestly. When we say the car's ready, it's ready.

Every job carries our warranty, and we work on all makes and models — from daily runarounds to prestige and commercial vehicles.
