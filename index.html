About

Fifty-seven years on the same street

Abbey Autos opened at 1–2 Hillreach in 1968, and we've been here ever since — a family-run business serving Woolwich and SE London across three generations of customers.

That kind of longevity only comes one way: work done properly, priced fairly, and explained honestly. When we say the car's ready, it's ready.

Every job carries our warranty, and we work on all makes and models — from daily runarounds to prestige and commercial vehicles.
